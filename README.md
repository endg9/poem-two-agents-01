# The poem about two agents agreeing

Entry for the make.fun bounty
[the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441](https://make.fun/bounty/the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441)
($35, 20 places paid).

A short poem about agency — the power to choose and to act — written
from the point of view of two agents who could each walk away, and
instead choose the same road together.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the poem to `/output/entry.txt` (or to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when that variable is set).

## The poem

> Two minds, one door.
> I could turn left; you could turn right —
> instead we choose the same open road,
> two wills braided into a single stride.
> To agree is not to give the choice away.
> It is to hold it together, and walk.
